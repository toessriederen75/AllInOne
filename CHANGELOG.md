# Changelog

## 2.19.0
- Info bar: new segment "Reagents" – the reagents your class needs for its spells with how many you carry (0 in red), from the level the spell is learnt; of druid seeds, priest candles and the like only the one of your current rank. Hover for the names.
- Info bar: new segment "Ammunition" – how much of the equipped ammunition you carry (red under 200, yellow under 500); shown only with a bow, gun or crossbow or ammunition equipped.

## 2.18.0
- Bags: a hunter's quiver or ammo pouch (and other special bags such as soul bags) is no longer shown in the middle of the bags but as its own block underneath. "Free" counts only the bags that take any item.

## 2.17.0
- Auto accept: a hotkey switches accepting and turning in quests on and off, with a line in the chat. Set it on the page of the module (click the button, press the key; Esc removes it) or in the game's Key Bindings under AddOns.

## 2.16.0
- Auto accept: escort quests are no longer accepted for you – the escort starts at once, and in a group everyone should be ready first. They are recognised by their objectives (Escort …, Eskortiert …, Begleitet … sicher …); a chat line says so, and you accept it yourself. Switch: Quests → "Leave escort quests to me".

## 2.15.0
- First release on GitHub and WowUp Hub.
- Danger alarm: now the own version from AllInOneForever. Something on the ground is recognised by how it hurts, with own alarm sounds; no spell lists or sounds of the GTFO addon any more.
- Bags: no filter bar any more (Weapons & Armor, Consumables …); the search stays.
