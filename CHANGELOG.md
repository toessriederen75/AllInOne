# Changelog

## 2.16.0
- Auto accept: escort quests are no longer accepted for you – the escort starts at once, and in a group everyone should be ready first. They are recognised by their objectives (Escort …, Eskortiert …, Begleitet … sicher …); a chat line says so, and you accept it yourself. Switch: Quests → "Leave escort quests to me".

## 2.15.0
- First release on GitHub and WowUp Hub.
- Danger alarm: now the own version from AllInOneForever. Something on the ground is recognised by how it hurts, with own alarm sounds; no spell lists or sounds of the GTFO addon any more.
- Bags: no filter bar any more (Weapons & Armor, Consumables …); the search stays.
