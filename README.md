# AllInOne

**Bags, bank, guild bank and everyday helpers in one addon – for WoW TBC Anniversary.**
*Taschen, Bank, Gildenbank und viele Alltagshelfer in einem Addon – für WoW TBC Anniversary.*

One addon instead of a dozen, written to use as little memory and CPU as possible. Every part can be switched on and off on its own (`/aio options`). All windows share one look, after Blizzard's modern interface.

## Features

- **Bags & Bank** – one window each for bags, bank and guild bank, with search, sorting, item level, quality borders, new items, item counts per character in tooltips and your other characters' items (offline view).
- **Merchants & NPCs** – sell junk, repair, keep reagents and ammunition stocked, accept and turn in quests, skip gossip.
- **Buffs** – a row of your missing own buffs (click to cast), consumables tracker.
- **Interface** – info bar (gold, durability, bag space, clock …), tooltips with IDs and item level, XP text, chat improvements, minimap button collector.
- **Spell ranks** – finds spells on your bars that are not at their highest rank and replaces them with one click.
- **Outfits** – save and wear sets of gear.
- **Cast bar** – an own cast bar with latency, ticks and timer bars for breath and fatigue.
- **Danger alarm** – a sound when you stand in something that hurts, for fatigue, drowning and being attacked while away.
- **Cleansing** – squares for your group that light up when someone has a debuff you can remove; click to cure.
- **Maps** – the world map as a movable window, dungeon entrances, flight masters, boats and portals on the map, the battlefield map.
- **Larger Blizzard windows** – quest log, professions and trainer as wide windows, with quest levels and "train all".
- **Comfort** – mail helpers, error filter, fast loot and other small automations.

Slash commands: `/aio` (bags), `/aio bank`, `/aio guild`, `/aio sort`, `/aio options`, `/aio find <text>`, `/aio forget <name>`.

## Installation

- **WowUp:** search for *AllInOne* in *Get Addons* (WowUp Hub).
- **By hand:** download the zip from [Releases](../../releases), unpack it into `World of Warcraft\_anniversary_\Interface\AddOns`.

Switch off Bagnon, Decursive and similar addons that do the same – otherwise two addons do the same job.
